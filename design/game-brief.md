# Game Brief — AFTERFRAME: THE LAST LOOP

## Elevator Pitch
**AFTERFRAME: THE LAST LOOP** is a single-player third-person 3D dark industrial sci-fi action-puzzle RPG set aboard the damaged orbital research station **Nadir-9**. Engineer **Sera Venn** uses her **Chrono Rig** to record short sequences of her own movement and combat actions and spawn **Echoes** that replay them independently in real time.

> *"Your past is your greatest weapon. Your future is the one thing WARDEN cannot predict."*

## Core Pillars
1. **Temporal Self-Cooperation (Chrono Rig Echoes)**: Record movement and attacks (`[T]`), spawn independent Echoes (`[Y]`), teleport via **Relay** (`[V]`), and trigger **Resonance Convergence** by following an Echo Heavy strike with a live Heavy strike.
2. **Tactical Four-Weapon Arsenal**: Switch dynamically between **Chrono Edge** (`[1]`), **Pulsecaster** (`[2]`), **Null-Point Rail Lance** (`[3]` — 1.75x exposed-core multiplier), and **Grav-Breaker Maul** (`[4]` — 85% frontal shield penetration), each with 2 modular upgrades.
3. **Readable Counterplay & Five Sovereign Wardens**: Fight 9 distinct enemy archetypes (including **Aegis Sentinel** and **Null Weaver**) and 5 multi-phase bosses (**HELIX-04**, **THE CHOIR**, **THE CURATOR**, **MATRON-V**, and **WARDEN PRIME**) across a 6-chapter campaign culminating in 3 Chronicle Loom endings (**SILENCE**, **WITNESS**, **CONTINUITY**).

## Target Platform & Tech Stack
- **Platform**: Web Browser (Desktop `1280x800+` Keyboard/Mouse & Mobile `390x844+` Touch)
- **Engine / Stack**: React 19 + TypeScript 7.0 + Vite 8 + Three.js 0.186.1 (`WebGLRenderer` + deterministic simulation in `src/game/simulation.ts`)
