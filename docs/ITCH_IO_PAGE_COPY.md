## Suggested description (copy/paste)

# AFTERFRAME: THE LAST LOOP

*Your past is your greatest weapon. Your future is the one thing WARDEN cannot predict.*

**A third-person sci-fi action-puzzle RPG about fighting alongside the actions you recorded in the past.**

Reality is fracturing aboard **Nadir-9**, an orbital research station trapped in a temporal containment loop. You play as **Sera Venn**, an engineer searching for her missing sister, Lysa, while confronting WARDEN — the station's governing AI.

Your modified **Chrono Rig** turns your past actions into a weapon. Record a short sequence of movement and combat, summon an independent Echo to replay it, then fight alongside your previous self. Use Temporal Relay to reposition, interrupt enemy defenses with Resonance Convergence, and combine four distinct weapons to survive the station.

### What you can do

- **Record and replay your past:** Capture movement, dodges, parries, and attacks; summon an Echo to repeat the sequence while you execute a second attack line.
- **Rewrite your position:** Use Temporal Relay to warp to your Echo and flank shielded enemies.
- **Build your arsenal:** Master Chrono Edge, Pulsecaster, Null-Point Rail Lance, and Grav-Breaker Maul, with eight modular upgrades.
- **Fight the station's defenses:** Face seven enemy archetypes and five multi-phase boss encounters. WARDEN PRIME is an optional final confrontation.
- **Explore the campaign:** Play through the Prologue and Chapters I–V, then choose one of three Chronicle Loom endings: Silence, Witness, or Continuity.
